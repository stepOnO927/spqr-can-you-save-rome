# Development card source audit

The campaign starts in 49 BCE. A card represents learning, local adaptation, or organization, not the first invention of its subject. Game costs and effects are balance values, not measurements from the sources. The drawings are original interpretive diagrams, not excavated objects.

All 34 material cards and 34 idea cards now have a direct source review. The historical links support specific practices and examples, not the game's numerical effects. Several ideas are deliberate policy abstractions rather than institutions attested in 49 BCE. Before changing another card, check the date, location, and the exact claim in both languages. Caesar's account is contemporary but written by a participant with his own political interest; it is not independent archaeological confirmation.
