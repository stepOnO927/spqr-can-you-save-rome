# Development card source audit

The campaign starts in 49 BCE. A card represents learning, local adaptation, or organization, not the first invention of its subject. Game costs and effects are balance values, not measurements from the sources. The drawings are original interpretive diagrams, not excavated objects.

The card-art path in `tech-art.js` creates inline SVG from local path data; it does not embed remote images or copied source-page illustrations. A catalog check covers all 84 technology and idea diagrams and 33 building diagrams: each renders, each is distinct within its group, and every card has English and Chinese context plus a source URL. The source websites are links for reading, not bundled art. This checks the development catalog's implementation and stated provenance; the map, character portraits, and Legacy assets require their separate rights records.

Commissioning instruction is a game transaction, not a claim that every listed technique was taught by a documented 49 BCE envoy. The selected subject must be attested in the partner's game knowledge and separately researched by the receiving country; each subject's card retains its own evidence and dating limits below.

## Merchant contact and research leads

[Polybius, *Histories* 3.22–26](https://penelope.uchicago.edu/Thayer/E/Roman/Texts/Polybius/3%2A.html) preserves much earlier Rome–Carthage treaty language on where merchants could trade; 3.22.8–9 specifically describes witnessed sales and state-backed payment. It establishes that maritime commerce involved personal contact and political limits. It does **not** document transfer of any named engineering technique, economic policy or cultural practice to a Roman state in 49 BCE. A merchant research lead is therefore a simulation possibility, never a historical claim about a specific game route. It requires paid foreign cargo to reach the buyer in two distinct months with adequate security. Eligible material techniques exclude guarded military knowledge; eligible ideas are public civilian practices already known to the seller, including visible government procedures, with the buyer's prerequisites intact. Military doctrines, court informant patronage and maritime intelligence are excluded. The lead expires and discounts one paid research project; it neither grants knowledge nor adopts an idea. An idea from another culture still needs its local support before adoption. The two-shipment threshold, six-month life, 3-treasury and one-month reductions are game balance values.
