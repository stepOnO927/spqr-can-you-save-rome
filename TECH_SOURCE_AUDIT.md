# Development card source audit

The campaign starts in 49 BCE. A card represents learning, local adaptation, or organization, not the first invention of its subject. Game costs and effects are balance values, not measurements from the sources. The drawings are original interpretive diagrams, not excavated objects.

A surviving bridge or marker is not necessarily dated to 49 BCE. |

The remaining cards still need a direct source review. A broad source link should be treated as background, especially where the evidence is Imperial rather than Republican. Before changing another card, check the date, location, and the exact claim in both languages. Caesar's account is contemporary but written by a participant with his own political interest; it is not independent archaeological confirmation.
