# Legacy city illustration

`assets/republican-rome-city.webp` is an original AI-generated, interpretive backdrop made with the built-in imagegen tool. It is not an archaeological reconstruction or a surveyed map of Rome in 49 BCE. Unlockable landmarks, their positions and their open/foundation states are separate game UI.

Final prompt:

> Historical-scene production asset for a strategy game's persistent Rome city hub, wide landscape 3:2 composition. Oblique aerial painted cartographic view of Republican Rome around 49 BCE: warm limestone and terracotta rooftops, compact ancient street blocks, small workshops and courtyards, a broad pale stone forum plaza near the center, the winding Tiber visible along the far right edge with a modest stone bridge, seven low hills suggested by topography, distant hazy countryside. Detailed but controlled hand-painted architectural model style, crisp roofs and legible roads, elegant aged parchment earth tones with muted olive greenery and blue-green river, soft late-afternoon light. Buildable landmarks will be overlaid separately by code, so keep the forum and several open parcels clear and avoid any monumental focal building that would conflict with them. No Colosseum, no Imperial-era triumphal monuments, no domes, no modern buildings, no fantasy, no people as focal subjects, no labels, no text, no logos, no interface elements. The visual should feel like a beautifully crafted isometric city plan rather than a photographic satellite image.

The 1536 × 1024 generated PNG was converted locally to WebP quality 84 for the bundled game asset (585,428 bytes). The original generated PNG is not required at runtime.

The Museum's bronze senatorial decree icon is original inline SVG artwork. It is a schematic plaque with abstract incised lines, not a copy or transcription of an ancient inscription. The exhibit text is informed by the [Kunsthistorisches Museum's Bacchanalia bronze tablet record](https://artsandculture.google.com/asset/senatus-consultum-de-bacchanalibus/zAEr3v_5qUsieA). That earlier object is presented as a teaching reference, not as an artifact from the game's 49 BCE campaign.
