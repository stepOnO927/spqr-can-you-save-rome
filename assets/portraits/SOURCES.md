## Campaign-born Roman children

Four original fictional people were created using the built-in image generation tool on 2026-11-11. Each has one childhood image and a separately edited adult image of the same fictional person. These are invented game artwork, not historical likenesses. Files `born-m1`, `born-m2`, `born-f1` and `born-f2` have `-child` and `-adult` variants, each with a 512 px master and 128 px and 64 px derivatives; the full generated PNG originals are retained locally in `outputs/child-portrait-originals-20261111/`.

The prompts requested a single fictional Roman child aged roughly eight or nine, head and shoulders, plain period-inspired wool, painterly realism, warm side light and a dark olive background, without text, emblems or modern details. The first boy has dark curls and ivory wool; the second has short straight hair, gray-green eyes and brown wool; the first girl has braids and a muted red wrap; the second has wavy chestnut hair and a muted green wrap. Each adult image was generated by editing its own child portrait into a young adult while preserving facial identity and the same style. The imagery is illustrative: it does not model every age or condition.

A portrait pair is reserved to at most one eligible campaign-born Roman child per save, including after death. The saved assignment checks the person's recorded name, birth year, sex and parents before use; other or ambiguous identities remain silhouettes. Children under five remain silhouettes, then the childhood image is used until the adult image appears at 18. Foreign portraits are only visible when a completed meeting recorded that specific image, and this observation does not reveal present age or status. The pool is limited to two male and two female people; additional people retain silhouettes rather than reusing a face. Existing saves receive assignments only for identifiable children with recorded simulated births and known parents when time next advances.
