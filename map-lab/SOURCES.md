# Map Lab · data, licensing and limitations

This is an experimental scene, separate from the playable campaign. It uses a separate initial simulation state and can issue orders and advance months without changing campaign saves or replacing the current game. Its use of contemporary land cover is a geographic visualization, not evidence for 49 BCE vegetation or settlement. The marked Via Flaminia is a schematic connection between known places, not a surveyed archaeological route. City coordinates are approximate modern locations of ancient sites.
