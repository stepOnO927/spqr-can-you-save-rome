# SPQR — remaining work

This tracker records the state of the **independent development branch**. A working mechanic is not a claim that every phase directive has been fulfilled. The 49 BCE Roman campaign remains the main playable scenario.

## Implemented and checked

- **Phase XI reconnaissance:** scouts, probes, waiting, dated and uncertain reports, prebattle estimates, battle reviews, a War Council, neutral military density markers, and later campaign AI scouting. The Senate Standard 5,200-man opening is covered in English and Chinese browser smoke tests.
- **Phase XIV development:** two research branches with 34 material innovations and 25 national ideas, 59 distinct interpretive vector illustrations, five idea categories, construction, institutional slot expansion, knowledge purchase/sale/exchange, visiting scholars, and local religion policy. Roads, bridges, harbor storage, and coastal forts have simulation effects. Senate and Egypt have automated 36-month grand-campaign checks using opposed five-idea strategies. AI states can sell knowledge to each other and send four-month offers to the player; accepting, declining and expiry are save-compatible and never charge the player without consent. Guarded military methods require stronger relations in purchases, sales, and exchanges.
- **Phase XVIII naval core:** fleets, shipbuilding, officer assignments, sea combat, piracy, transport, maritime trade, and AI fleet orders. Patrols can fight pirate vessels; amphibious attacks use a disclosed battle penalty. AI can embark troops with actual transport ships. Trade payments now depend on cargo quantity, route length and scarcity, while raids move captured goods into the raider's inventory. A hostile blockade reduces an army's supply in the blockaded port.
- **Legacy achievements:** silent in active campaigns, revealed on formal conclusion or terminal defeat, with one-time reward keys. New campaigns have unique IDs; older imported saves receive a stable ID based on the imported file.
- **Regression:** `npm test` and browser smoke scripts for the atlas, Senate reconnaissance, development, and grand campaign.
- **Offline atlas:** MapLibre GL JS, its license, physical land and sea data, and 64 overview terrain tiles are bundled. A disconnected browser smoke test loads 120 settlement markers with a real map engine; higher-zoom terrain detail remains network-dependent.
- **Grand-campaign start fix:** Starting the tutorial or free play inside the atlas now creates the 120-region campaign, matching the main entry flow. Base-map city labels are suppressed in grand mode; the tutorial's highlighted city remains available. Major settlement labels have a darker, more legible cartographic style.

## Still incomplete

1. **Phase XIV source and art review.** Every technology card has a different original schematic drawing, not an excavated artifact. Nine material cards now link directly to checked sources; the first pass also corrected the misleading fortification and library labels while keeping their save IDs. See `TECH_SOURCE_AUDIT.md`. The remaining material cards and all ideas still need a direct source review. The 34/25 catalog is a playable slice; deeper policy, religious, cultural, and civilization-specific content remains unfinished.
2. **Phase XIV balance and long campaigns.** A no-order Caesar run on the 120-region map reached terminal defeat after 22 months in both desktop and mobile browser audits; this is an observed outcome, not a balance target. Senate and Egypt complete automated 36-month grand-campaign development paths. More manual play across factions and difficulties is needed without scripting a guaranteed result. Research, economy, faith, migration, and diplomacy need longer campaign review.
3. **Phase XVIII naval depth.** Piracy and sea battles still use aggregate fleet calculations. Player tactical choices, pirate cargo inventories, commander capture, a broader port-to-front maritime supply network, and more nuanced detection remain to be built and balanced. Trade prices and AI troop transports now work, but need long campaign balance testing.
4. **Phase XV map geography and visual review.** The 120 territories are gameplay cells based on imperfect historical sources. A current 1440-pixel browser capture exposed duplicate base-city labels on top of grand-campaign site labels; these are now hidden in grand campaign mode. Coastlines, borders, names, terrain, contrast, zoom behavior, small screens, and accessibility still need a cartographic review and user acceptance. This remains the user's principal visual complaint.
5. **Phase XVI Legacy hub depth.** The city, shop, museum, archive, race, and campaign rewards are playable, but building-specific activities and museum curation are narrower than the full directive.
6. **Phase XI continued playtesting.** One Senate Standard path and both languages are covered by browser smoke, plus targeted simulation tests. The atlas tutorial and free start now retain the grand campaign. More independent human campaigns, alternate decisions, long intelligence chains, AI mistakes, and save/import flows need playtesting. Local saves contain actual simulation state, so fog of war is a gameplay interface boundary, not file encryption.
7. **Historical audit.** Figures, politics, religion, regional boundaries, and economic values are approximations. Check source and license records before presenting any additional historical statement as fact.
