# SPQR — remaining work

This tracker records the state of the **independent development branch**. A working mechanic is not a claim that every phase directive has been fulfilled. The 49 BCE Roman campaign remains the main playable scenario.

## Implemented and checked

- **Phase XIV dam chronology correction, 23 November 2026:** A review of the Dam Engineering card found that its older UNESCO nomination source proposed a second-century BCE date for Spain's Alcantarilla dam, while the Castilla-La Mancha government's 2020 heritage declaration uses construction-layer finds to indicate a Flavian (69–96 CE) build. The card and building reference now use a published archaeological and engineering study of the Alyzia dam in western Greece, dated roughly to the mid-fourth to third century BCE. Both languages distinguish that local pre-49 BCE example from the game's reusable dam policy and invented harvest values. The source audit records the conflicting evidence, while the research/building IDs, costs, geography, maintenance and save behavior remain stable. The 100 focused development tests, both development-tree browser reviews and the complete bilingual Phase XIV research, adoption, construction and knowledge-trade browser flow pass. This corrects one concrete historical error; the wider catalog and source-by-source audit remain open.

- **Approximate ancient Lacus Fucinus, 23 November 2026:** The atlas now restores the inland lake missing from modern hydrography after the basin's drainage. Matched close-map screenshots show the former blank basin and the restored lake. This is one geographic correction and two small layout fixes, not completion of the wider historical-coast, map-art or long-campaign balance requests.
