# SPQR — remaining work

This tracker records the state of the **independent development branch**. A working mechanic is not a claim that every phase directive has been fulfilled. The 49 BCE Roman campaign remains the main playable scenario.

## Implemented and checked

- **Wartime-only trade raids and neutral cargo incidents:** A fleet can now order a trade raid only when at least one end of that route is controlled by a current enemy. Existing raid orders in imported saves are canceled when that condition ceases, and route settlement checks hostility again before any cargo can change hands. If a wartime raid seizes cargo from a neutral origin, the raider loses six relations with that origin and a dated diplomatic incident is recorded. The player sees the neutral-cargo risk in the raid selector and the incident in English and Chinese reports. A new save-and-peace regression, the real bilingual browser flow and the full 408-test suite pass. This closes one exploit; broader Phase XVIII naval balance and AI strategy remain open.
- **AI wartime replacement and retreat parity:** A territorially outnumbered AI with too little field strength can now pay the ordinary 24 treasury, 6 food and 3,000 manpower to reinforce or raise a unit on its normal four-month review even when its cash is below the former 65-treasury peace threshold. Arrival still takes a month. A blockaded, low-supply AI army now uses the same adjacent friendly retreat rule as the player, including the morale cost, loss of preparation and encirclement check, instead of changing its location directly. Targeted tests cover both. Five deterministic 36-month spectator campaigns ended with Caesar controlling 13, 11, 20, 26 and 7 territories; a comparison with the prior recruitment condition yielded 19, 15, 7, 15 and 8. The outcomes are mixed and the Senate remains favored; this does not settle balance. The fixed strategy audit and full suite passed.
- **Curia-to-Court appeal chain:** Dismissing Catinius at both the original Curia hearing and his return now opens a distinct Court of Appeals case once that building is owned. The court weighs a witness and a limited settlement rather than presenting a single objectively correct answer. Three rulings change different permanent political measures; the chosen judgment, date, one-time Laurel reward and fictional museum record survive profile export and reload. The special appeal is shown before ordinary court exercises and remains unavailable after any other Curia path. A focused test covers its branch, effects, reload and reward guard; the real browser path covers both languages, the shop unlock, court, museum and repeat-load behavior. Visual inspection exposed a stale Chinese purchase notice on the English court screen; switching languages now clears that short-lived notice. Other Legacy buildings and longer consequence chains remain incomplete.
- **Close-scale political tint cleanup:** At Italian regional zoom, the synthetic 120-cell catchment polygons formed straight, faction-colored wedges across continuous mountain relief and the coast. Their fill now fades away by zoom 7.4 in political, military and maritime views, while faction-colored macro borders, settlement banners and army standards remain. Terrain and statistic overlays also retire the synthetic cells at close zoom. The offline atlas browser path passed, and a screenshot taken after elevation tiles settled shows the Apennines without those colored wedges. The screenshot test now waits longer when its optional zoom-7 capture is requested, preventing an incomplete tile load from being mistaken for a flat map. Broader cartographic quality remains open.
- **AI transport staging:** An AI with an eligible army at a friendly port and a hostile port across a real sea link can now move a sufficiently capacious fleet from another friendly port or an idle sea position to meet it. It waits at embarkation long enough for the army to order the existing paid, vulnerable crossing instead of immediately accepting a patrol. The AI uses only its own army, political control and dated naval sightings; a strong reported threat on the staging route or insufficient funds prevents the move. Targeted tests cover the dated report, budget, save reload, fleet arrival and a four-month live crossing without army teleportation. A regression exposed and fixed a damaged-fleet bug in the new order priority: a fleet already docked for repair no longer departs merely to "return" to that same port. The fixed 36-month audit remains valid but shifts slightly: the Egypt spectator route now ends with Caesar 16 and Senate 84 territories, and the Egypt active route sees a Senate civil-war resolution. The Senate advantage remains a balance debt.
- **Share tenancy arrangements:** A second sourced estate policy now offers a real alternative to restrictive labor control. After estate organization, research and timed adoption, it improves quarterly satisfaction and loyalty only in productive rural territories and permits up to 225 distressed people to relocate from each such territory per month. In exchange, a limited share of the harvest leaves public food stores and the policy has monthly upkeep. The two policies conflict but may replace each other in their own idea slot after the normal transition and stability cost. Cato 136–137 supports the existence of particular share tenancy terms, not universal freedom or the numerical effects. English and Chinese cards, a distinct drawing, the monthly budget, save reload and simulation tests cover the policy. Wider agricultural and long-run balance remain incomplete.
